# Supplementary Scanner Report Identifiers

These identifiers document the RQ4 scanner sanity check. They are not a
store-vetting experiment and should not be interpreted as evidence that Google
Play or the Apple App Store would accept the submitted packages. Scanner
classifications are service-specific and may reflect packaging, reputation, or
policy heuristics unrelated to the PHANTOM metadata mechanism. The narrow
observation is that public multi-scanner services did not consistently provide
mechanism-specific detection of the oversized-metadata, no-code attack surface
across the representative samples.

MetaDefender report URLs may use either hash-based result pages or upload-result
pages, depending on which public result page is available for a submitted
sample.
